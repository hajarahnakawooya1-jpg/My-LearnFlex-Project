Welcome to LernFlex! Let’s Learn Together!

Hello, friends! 👋 I am your teacher today, and we are going to explore exciting things about letters, numbers, science, art, and the world around us. Are you ready? Let’s go! 🚀

1. Language & Reading Fun

Learn the Alphabets
  • The alphabet has 26 letters: A, B, C… all the way to Z.
  • Letters have big forms (uppercase) and small forms (lowercase).

Activity: Can you find all the letters in your room? Maybe on books, toys, or your clothes!

Letter Sounds (Phonics)

Every letter has a sound.

  • A says “ah” like in apple.
  • B says “buh” like in ball.

Activity: Try saying the sounds with me: A, B, C… Can you think of a word for each letter?

Learn New Words
  • Every day, try to learn 5 new words.
  • Words tell stories and help you talk to friends and family.

Activity: Pick a fruit and say its name. Can you spell it too?

Reading Stories
  • Reading is like going on an adventure!
  • Start with short stories or picture books.

Activity: After reading, tell me: Who is the main character? What happened first?

Writing Practice

Writing letters, words, and sentences makes your brain strong!

Activity: Take a pencil and write the word cat. Draw a picture of it too.

2. Numbers & Math

Counting Numbers
  • Numbers help us know “how many.”
  • Let’s count together: 1, 2, 3… 10!

Activity: Count your toys or pencils. How many do you have?

Adding & Subtracting
  • Adding (+) means putting things together.
  • Subtracting (-) means taking things away.

Example: You have 3 apples 🍎🍎🍎. You eat 1 🍎. How many are left? 3 - 1 = 2

Activity: Try with your toys at home!

Shapes and Colors
  • 2D shapes: Circle, square, triangle.
  • 3D shapes: Cube, sphere, cylinder.
  • are everywhere — look around you!

Activity: Find 3 shapes in your room and name them.

Patterns and Sequences

Patterns are things that repeat.

Patterns are things that repeat.

Example: red, blue, red, blue… what comes next? Red!

Activity: Make your own pattern with your crayons or toys.

3. Science & Nature

Animals & Plants
  • Animals live in different places: forests, farms, oceans. Plants grow from seeds into flowers, fruits, and trees.

Activity: Plant a seed in a cup and watch it grow!

The Human Body
  • We have a body that helps us move, see, hear, and think.

    • Head helps us think, eyes help us see, hands help us touch.

    Activity: Point to your eyes, nose, ears, and fingers.

    Weather & Seasons
    • Sometimes it is sunny ☀, rainy 🌧, or snowy ❄.
    • Seasons change: Summer, Autumn, Winter, Spring.

    Activity: Look outside — what season is it today?

    Simple Experiments

    Science is fun when we try experiments!

    Example: Mix red and blue paint — what color do you get? Purple!

    Activity: Drop a small toy in water. Does it float or sink?

    Creativity & Arts

    Drawing & Coloring

    Draw what you see, or what you imagine!

    Activity: Draw your favorite animal and color it.

    Crafts & DIY

    Use paper, glue, and colors to make something special.

    Activity: Fold a paper into a hat or make a paper boat.

    Music & Dance

    Sing songs and dance to the rhythm!

    Activity: Clap your hands to your favorite nursery rhyme.

    Drama & Storytelling

    Pretend you are a character from a story.

    Activity: Act like a lion roaring or a bird flying!

    5. Life Skills & Social Fun

    Hygiene Habits
    • Wash your hands before eating and after playing.
    • Brush your teeth every morning and night.

    Activity: Make a handwashing song!

    Healthy Eating

    Eat fruits, vegetables, and drink water to stay strong.

    Activity: Draw your favorite healthy meal.

    Feelings & Emotions

    We all feel happy 😊, sad 😢, or angry 😠 sometimes.

    Talk about your feelings to a friend or parent.

    Activity: Draw a face showing how you feel today.

    Safety & Environment
    • Don’t touch dangerous things and look both ways when crossing the street.
    • Take care of nature — plant trees and recycle.

    Activity: Pick up trash around your home and make it a clean place!

    6. Fun Games & Learning

    • Solve puzzles 🧩
    • Match colors, numbers, or letters
    • Play memory games with cards
    • Dance and move your body to learn while having fun

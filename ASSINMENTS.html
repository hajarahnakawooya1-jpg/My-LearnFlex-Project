ASSIGNMETS FOR KIDS

ENGLISH ACTIVITY

Topic: Writing and Imagination

Assignment 1:

  • Write five sentences about your best friend.
  • Say their name, what they like, what you do together, and why you like them.

Assignment 2:

  • Write three short sentences using these words:
  • play, school, happy.

Assignment 3:

  • Arrange these words to make a correct sentence:
  • like / I / reading / books.
  • eats / cat / my / fish.

MATHEMATICS ACTIVITY

Topic: Numbers and Operations

Assignment 1:

  • Solve these sums:
  • 5 + 8 = ?
  • 14 – 9 = ?
  • 6 × 3 = ?
  • 20 ÷ 4 = ?

Assignment 2:

  • Count the number of doors and windows in your classroom or home.
  • Write how many they are in total.

Assignment 3:

  • Write all the numbers from 1 to 50 in your exercise book.
  • Circle the even numbers.

🔬 SCIENCE ACTIVITY

Topic: Living and Non-living Things

Assignment 1:

  • Write three differences between living and non-living things.
  • Example: Living things can move, grow, and breathe.

Assignment 2:

Draw and name two animals that live in water and two that live on land.

Assignment 3:

  • Name five parts of a plant and tell their work.
  • >Hint: Root, stem, leaf, flower, fruit.*

🌍 ENVIRONMENT STUDIES

Topic: Our Surroundings

Assignment 1:

  • Write three things that make the environment dirty.

Assignment 2:

  • Write four ways we can protect trees.

Assignment 3:

  • Observe your school compound or home and list things that are clean and those that are dirty.
  • Tell what should be done to make it clean.

💪 HEALTH EDUCATION

Topic: Personal Hygiene

Assignment 1:

  • List five items you use to keep your body clean.
  • Example: Soap, towel, toothbrush, toothpaste, comb.*

Assignment 2:

  • Write two times in a day when you should wash your hands.

Assignment 3:

    Explain why eating healthy food is important.

🎨 ART AND CREATIVITY

Topic: Drawing and Colouring

Assignment 1:

  • Draw your favorite fruit and color it beautifully.

Assignment 2:

  • Make a drawing showing you helping your parents at home.

Assignment 3:

  • Use colored pencils or crayons to draw your classroom.
  • Label things like the teacher’s table, blackboard, and chairs.

🎶 MUSIC AND FUN

Topic: Singing and Rhythm

Assignment 1:

  • Sing your favorite nursery rhyme to your parent or teacher.
  • Some ideas: “Baa Baa Black Sheep,” “If You’re Happy and You Know It.”

Assignment 2:

  • Clap your hands and make your own short song using your name.

Assignment 3:

  • Dance to one song for 5 minutes and tell how you felt after dancing.

🧠 THINKING AND LOGIC

Topic: Observation and Reasoning

Assignment 1:

  • Look around your home and name five things that use electricity.

Assignment 2:

  • Name three things that float on water and three that sink.

Assignment 3:

  • You see a child crying — what would you do to help?
  • Write your answer in one or two sentences.

📚 READING CORNER

Topic: Reading and Comprehension

Assignment 1:

  • Read any short story or poem.
  • After reading, tell someone what the story was about.

Assignment 2:

  • Read these words and write a short sentence for each:
    • sun, rain, tree, bird, school.

    Assignment 3:

    • Find five words around your home that start with the letter B.

    💬 MORAL EDUCATION

    Topic: Good Behavior and Manners

    Assignment 1:

    • Write five polite words we should use every day.
    • Hint: Please, Sorry, Thank you, Excuse me, You’re welcome.

    Assignment 2:

    • Write three ways of showing respect to your parents and teachers.

    Assignment 3:

    • Write a short story about helping a friend or sharing something you have.

    🏫 SOCIAL STUDIES

    Topic: People Who Help Us

    Assignment 1:

    • Write the names of five people who help in the community.
    • Example: Doctor, Teacher, Farmer, Police Officer, Driver.

    Assignment 2:

    • Write what a farmer does.

    Assignment 3:

    • Draw a picture of a teacher in class and color it neatly.

    💡 BONUS ACTIVITIES

    • Find and write 10 words you can see around you (like bag, pen, desk, chair, shoe, cup).
    • Find something red, something blue, and something yellow in your room.
    • Ask your parent or guardian to tell you a story.
    • After listening, draw a picture showing what happened in the story.
    • Do one kind thing for someone this week.
    • Write what you did and how it made you feel.
